## Shared assessment stages
- HR: motivation, communication, ownership, collaboration and expectations.
- Technical: programme-specific concepts and applied reasoning.
- Practical: task brief, constraints, submission and rubric; coding environment only for relevant tracks.
- Communication: clarity, listening, explanation and structured thinking.

## Scorecard
Score each criterion on an anchored 1–5 rubric. Require evidence notes for material ratings. Keep interviewer scores and recommendations separate from AI-generated summaries. The interviewer owns the final recommendation.
